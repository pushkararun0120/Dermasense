# Dermasense – Business Model Canvas

## 1. Introduction

Dermasense is a hydrogel-based wound patch designed for the early detection of wound infection using a pH-based colour change. By combining hydrogel technology that supports faster healing with a chemical pH-indicator system, the patch provides a simple, low-cost, and easy-to-use wound-monitoring solution that operates safely and reliably without requiring any electronics, batteries, or digital sensors.

The business model explores how Dermasense can be designed, validated, manufactured, distributed, and supplied to patients, clinicians, and healthcare facilities. It outlines the operational framework needed to reduce unnecessary hospital visits and lower the wound-monitoring burden for both healthcare professionals and patients recovering outside clinical settings.

The following canvas represents the complete business model for the Dermasense project, aligning clinical value propositions, customer segments, distribution channels, revenue models, and operational requirements.

---

## 2. Customer Segments

Customer segments define the specific patient groups, medical professionals, and healthcare organisations that use, recommend, or purchase Dermasense.

### Primary Patient and Caregiver Segments

- **Post-surgical patients (especially high-risk patients such as diabetic patients):** Individuals recovering from surgical procedures who face an elevated risk of post-operative wound complications or delayed healing and benefit from early visual warnings of infection.
- **Home healthcare providers:** Caregivers, visiting nurses, and home-care services that manage post-operative and chronic wound recovery in domestic settings and need straightforward visual monitoring tools.

### Clinical and Institutional Segments

- **Hospitals and surgical centers:** Acute-care institutions and surgical departments that manage high volumes of post-operative wounds and seek efficient dressings that reduce readmissions and outpatient follow-up burdens.
- **Doctors and surgeons:** Clinical decision-makers who oversee surgical recovery, prescribe wound-care regimens, and recommend reliable monitoring solutions to their patients.
- **Rural healthcare clinics:** Primary and community clinics in resource-limited or remote settings that require affordable, equipment-free wound-assessment tools without reliance on electronic diagnostic hardware.

Focusing on both institutional clinical buyers and home-recovery environments ensures Dermasense addresses the full continuum of post-surgical and high-risk wound care.

---

## 3. Value Propositions

The value proposition explains why patients, doctors, and healthcare facilities would choose Dermasense over standard dressings or complex electronic wound sensors.

### Core Clinical and Practical Benefits

- **Early detection of wound infection using pH-based colour change:** A chemical pH-indicator integrated into the patch visibly shifts colour in response to changes in wound-fluid pH, alerting patients and clinicians to early signs of infection before severe symptoms develop.
- **Supports faster healing with hydrogel technology:** The hydrogel matrix maintains a moist, soothing, and biocompatible wound environment that actively promotes tissue regeneration and faster recovery.
- **Reduces hospital visits and monitoring burden:** Clear visual feedback enables patients and home caregivers to check wound status without removing the dressing prematurely or making unnecessary trips to the hospital for routine inspections.
- **Simple, low-cost, and easy-to-use solution:** The integrated patch format is applied just like a standard dressing and requires no specialised technical training to interpret.
- **No electronics required (safe and reliable):** By relying entirely on a chemical indicator and hydrogel matrix rather than batteries, wires, or wireless transmitters, the patch eliminates electronic failure modes and remains safe for direct skin contact.

Together, these value propositions combine active wound care with accessible visual monitoring in a single disposable patch.

---

## 4. Channels

Channels describe how Dermasense reaches target customer segments and how healthcare providers and patients access the product.

### Primary Clinical and Commercial Channels

- **Hospitals and clinics (primary distribution):** Direct supply to hospital procurement teams, surgical wards, and outpatient wound-care clinics serves as the foundational distribution pathway.
- **Direct partnership with healthcare providers:** Collaborative agreements with hospital networks, surgical centers, and rural health systems to integrate Dermasense into standard post-operative care protocols.

### Retail, Wholesale, and Digital Channels

- **Medical supply distributors and pharmacies:** Partnerships with established medical wholesalers and retail pharmacies to ensure steady local availability for clinics, doctors, and discharged patients.
- **Online healthcare platforms:** Digital medical supply stores and e-pharmacy platforms that allow home healthcare providers, clinics, and individual patients to order patches directly.

Using hospital distribution as the primary channel builds clinical credibility while pharmacies and online platforms ensure continuity of care after patient discharge.

---

## 5. Customer Relationships

Customer relationships outline how Dermasense builds clinical trust, supports proper product usage, and maintains long-term engagement with medical professionals and patients.

### Clinical and Patient Engagement Approach

- **Doctor-recommended usage:** Establishing trust through physician and surgeon endorsement, ensuring patients use the patch under appropriate clinical guidance as part of their prescribed recovery plan.
- **Training and awareness for hospital staff:** Conducting onboarding sessions, demonstrations, and reference workshops for nurses, surgical teams, and clinical staff on proper patch application and colour-scale interpretation.
- **Post-sale support and guides:** Providing clear usage manuals, colour-reference charts, and responsive support channels for hospitals, clinics, and individual buyers after purchase.
- **Educational content for patients:** Supplying accessible educational materials that explain how to observe pH colour changes at home, when to change the dressing, and when to contact a doctor.

This relationship model connects professional clinical oversight with clear, reassuring guidance for patients recovering at home.

---

## 6. Revenue Streams

Revenue streams define the commercial pathways through which Dermasense generates income across retail, institutional, and B2B markets.

### Commercial Revenue Models

- **Sale of Dermasense patches (one-time purchase):** Direct sales of individual patches or multi-patch recovery packs to patients, pharmacies, and clinics for short-term post-surgical recovery.
- **Bulk sale to hospitals:** High-volume supply contracts with hospitals, surgical centers, and healthcare networks for routine use across surgical and wound-care departments.
- **Subscription model for regular supply:** Recurring delivery plans tailored for chronic wound management, diabetic care programmes, home-healthcare agencies, and rural clinics requiring uninterrupted stock.
- **Licensing technology to medical companies:** Intellectual property and technology-licensing agreements that allow established medical-dressing manufacturers to integrate Dermasense's pH-responsive hydrogel system into their own product lines.

### Pricing Considerations

Product pricing across these streams is structured around:

- Raw material costs for the hydrogel matrix and chemical pH indicators.
- Manufacturing, quality control, and sterile packaging expenses.
- Testing, validation, and regulatory certification overheads.
- Distribution, logistics, and clinical marketing margins.

Combining direct product sales with recurring subscriptions and B2B licensing creates a diversified and scalable revenue foundation.

---

## 7. Key Resources

Key resources encompass the core technical assets, infrastructure, and human expertise required to develop, produce, and commercialise Dermasense.

### Technical and Material Resources

- **Hydrogel material technology:** The biocompatible polymer formulation engineered to absorb wound exudate, maintain moisture balance, and support faster tissue healing.
- **pH indicator chemical system:** The colour-responsive chemical sensing system calibrated to shift visibly within the physiological pH range associated with wound infection.

### Operational and Human Resources

- **Manufacturing setup:** Dedicated fabrication, assembly, and sterile packaging equipment capable of producing consistent medical-grade hydrogel patches.
- **Research and development team:** Multidisciplinary scientists, materials engineers, and product developers responsible for formulation optimisation, stability testing, and product innovation.

These four core resources form the technical and operational backbone of the Dermasense project.

---

## 8. Key Activities

Key activities represent the critical operational, technical, and commercial tasks required to bring Dermasense from laboratory formulation to clinical deployment.

### Core Operational Tasks

- **Product design and development:** Formulating the hydrogel layer, integrating the pH-indicator chemistry, and engineering a practical, user-friendly patch architecture.
- **Testing and validation of pH detection:** Conducting rigorous laboratory and simulated wound-fluid evaluations to verify colour-change accuracy, response speed, repeatability, and non-leaching safety.
- **Manufacturing and quality control:** Establishing repeatable production processes and strict batch-testing protocols to ensure every patch meets medical safety and performance standards.
- **Marketing to hospitals and doctors:** Engaging surgeons, wound-care specialists, and hospital procurement teams through clinical outreach, demonstrations, and medical awareness campaigns.
- **Distribution and supply chain management:** Coordinating raw chemical and hydrogel procurement, sterile inventory storage, and timely delivery across hospitals, distributors, pharmacies, and online platforms.

Executing these activities ensures both technical reliability in the lab and smooth adoption in clinical practice.

---

## 9. Key Partnerships

Key partnerships include the external institutions, suppliers, manufacturers, and authorities that support Dermasense's production, compliance, and market reach.

### Strategic Partner Network

- **Hospitals and healthcare providers:** Clinical partners that collaborate on product evaluation, clinical feedback, staff training, and primary patient distribution.
- **Medical device manufacturers:** Industrial production partners that provide scalable fabrication capacity, sterile assembly expertise, and potential licensing collaborations.
- **Raw material suppliers (hydrogel and chemicals):** Specialised chemical and biomaterial vendors supplying medical-grade hydrogel polymers and high-purity pH indicator reagents.
- **Regulatory bodies:** Healthcare and medical-device authorities responsible for reviewing safety data, granting device certifications, and overseeing compliance standards.
- **Distributors and pharmacies:** Wholesale medical supply networks and retail pharmacy chains that expand product accessibility across urban and rural regions.

Leveraging these partnerships reduces capital overhead while accelerating regulatory approval and clinical distribution.

---

## 10. Cost Structure

The cost structure outlines the major financial expenditures involved in researching, certifying, manufacturing, and delivering Dermasense.

### Production and Technical Costs

- **Manufacturing cost (hydrogel + indicator):** Direct costs of raw hydrogel polymers, chemical pH indicators, substrate materials, patch fabrication, and assembly labour.
- **Research and development:** Ongoing investment in formulation refinement, material compatibility studies, prototype iteration, and R&D personnel.
- **Testing and certification:** Expenses for laboratory validation of pH detection, biocompatibility testing, sterilisation verification, and regulatory certification filings.

### Commercial and Operational Costs

- **Marketing and distribution:** Costs associated with hospital outreach, doctor awareness programmes, staff training materials, and distributor channel management.
- **Packaging and logistics:** Expenses for medical-grade sterile packaging, shelf-life protection, warehousing, and cold/ambient supply-chain transport.

Maintaining a non-electronic, chemical-based patch architecture helps keep unit manufacturing costs low while focusing investment on quality validation and clinical reach.

## 12. Conclusion

The Dermasense Business Model Canvas establishes a practical roadmap for translating a pH-responsive hydrogel wound patch into a scalable healthcare solution. By combining faster hydrogel-assisted healing with early visual infection detection, the product directly addresses the needs of post-surgical and high-risk diabetic patients while easing the monitoring burden on doctors, hospitals, home caregivers, and rural clinics.

Because the patch requires no electronics, it remains simple, safe, reliable, and cost-effective to manufacture. Supported by a diversified revenue model—spanning one-time purchases, bulk hospital contracts, recurring subscriptions, and technology licensing—and backed by strategic partnerships with manufacturers, suppliers, clinicians, and regulatory bodies, Dermasense is positioned for sustainable clinical and commercial impact.
